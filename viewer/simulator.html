Controls & model

Right stick: steering and pitch. Left slider: throttle. Arrow keys also move the stick; W/S adjust throttle. Release centers the stick. Throttle stays where you leave it.

MANUAL directly controls brakes and throttle. FBWA uses heading-rate steering with manual throttle. CRUISE controls heading and altitude; its pitch input requests climb/descent.

Launch uses ArduPlane TAKEOFF and the native throw assist. After reaching 55 m, the selected mode engages. Fly mission enters AUTO; choose another mode to take over.

Physics: firmware’s native rigid-body paraglider, not the separate 9-DOF research model. Canopy and brake deformation are illustrative. All control is local to this tab.
