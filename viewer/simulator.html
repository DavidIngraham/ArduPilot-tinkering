Controls & model

Right stick: steering and pitch. Left slider: throttle. Arrow keys also move the stick; W/S adjust throttle. Release centers the stick. Throttle stays where you leave it.

MANUAL directly controls brakes and throttle. FBWA uses heading-rate steering with manual throttle. CRUISE controls heading and altitude; its pitch input requests climb/descent.

Starts held on the ground while sensors initialize. The default circuit uploads and starts automatically in AUTO when sensors are ready. Choose another mode and Engage to take over.

Full 9-DOF canopy–payload research model, compiled from its C++ kernel. Both bodies, rotor and brake actuators are drawn from simulation truth. Uses the as-built 132 mm riser spacing and a fit to existing Hood River / Trout Lake logs. Ground contact uses a flat plane; overhead inflation and hand launch are assisted approximations, not a cloth pull-up simulation. Launch timing and friction are assumptions. All control is local to this tab.
